## Issues (by severity)

1. **README contradicts itself on first-party names (low, doc fix).** `README.md:3` opens with "Original Game Boy (DMG) action-platformer" while the Header paragraph claims "no first-party names or art appear anywhere". "Game Boy" is a Nintendo trademark. The page, STATUS.md, the ROM text and `source/src` are clean; the word also appears in `CONTRACT.md:3,41`, `source/tools/pix.py:8`, `tools/sameboy_runner.c:2`, `tools/mgba_runner.c:2`. Suggest "1989 monochrome handheld (DMG)" as the page already does, or narrow the README claim to "ROM and page".
2. **PULSE/ROTARY belt lines sit at jump height and share the shade of the thin ledges (low, legibility).** In frames 4000/4200/4400 (plate 5) and 4950/5150 (plate 6) the dashed `BD_BELT_T/B` lines (`backdrop.py:135–136`) run across the band where the player jumps, right above the short striped stone ledges which are also colour 2. The ledges are thicker with a double edge, so on inspection they are distinguishable, and the rule "backdrops never look like phase blocks" holds, but a first-time player may try to land on a belt. A one-row gap between belts and the ledge band, or dropping `BD_BELT_B`, would remove the doubt. Not a blocker.
3. **Boss idle is subtle and arms barely read as arms (low, cosmetic).** The two idle frames differ by a pupil shift and a 1-px move of the side nubs (frames 6701 vs 6717). The silhouette, eyes, grin and drips carry the character; the "stubby arms" read as bumps. Meets the parent's ask (eyes, limbs, two-frame idle) but the idle is only visible when looking for it.
4. **Ink label updates one frame after the flip (negligible).** Trace 1253 has `g_phase`=1 with the B press, but the HUD still shows `I INK` in that frame's picture (also boss frame 6693). Human-invisible; noted because it looks like a film offset until you check the trace.
5. **Per-plate figures in `evidence/levels/levels.json` are from the per-plate runs, not the campaign run (informational).** e.g. plate 1 `play_frames` 670 / busy max 62.5 vs README 672 / 65.3; plate 8 busy max 57.1 vs 74.9 (the per-plate boss script differs from the campaign's). README labels its table "full campaign" so it is not wrong, but a reader cross-checking against levels.json will see different numbers; a one-line note in README would help.
6. **First two PLAY frames of plate 8 show no boss / a blank screen (6690 blank, 6691 no boss; negligible).** Display-off load tail inside the PLAY state; invisible at 60 Hz.
